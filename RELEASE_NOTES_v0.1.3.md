**New**
- Sheet Layout: every panel laid out in rows on 4 × 8 (or 5 × 5, 4 × 4, 2 × 4, or your size) plywood sheets with a saw-kerf gap, per thickness, printable, with the sheet count. The Cut List picks the sheet size.
- Notes and dimensions placed on a panel are printed on that part in the cut sheet and the sheet layout.
- Cabinet builder: overlay doors, a toe kick (notched sides and a kick board) or a drawer bank with a box behind every front; Entity Info › Edit Cabinet rebuilds a cabinet with new sizes where it stands. The sample model opens with a cabinet beside the house.
- Parts library: File › Import Model as Component brings another .gsk in as one component; Save Selected Component As writes a group out as its own .gsk; the Components panel can Replace selected groups with a component.
- Tape Measure resize: measure two points, type the real length, and the whole model scales to match.
- Move takes x,y,z steps and [x,y,z] positions; Scale takes a size with a unit (24", 600mm) instead of a factor.
- Edit › Make Faces: loose lines that close a flat loop become faces again.
- View › Color by Tag with a colour swatch per tag; a Snaps menu in the status bar to switch snap kinds off; a section fill that caps cut solids; an Outliner show / hide box per group.
- Cloud autosave for models kept in your account; smooth camera flights between views and scenes; the Eraser also takes dimensions, notes and guides; a welcome card on first run.
